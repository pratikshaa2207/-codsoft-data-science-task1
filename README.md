# -codsoft-data-science-task1
The sinking of the Titanic is one of the most notorious maritime disasters in history.

On April 15, 1912, during its first voyage, the so-called "unsinkable" RMS Titanic struck an iceberg and went down. Tragically, there were not enough lifeboats for everyone on board, leading to the deaths of 1,502 out of 2,224 passengers and crew.

Although chance played a role in survival, certain groups of people had a higher likelihood of surviving than others.

In this challenge, your task is to develop a predictive model that explores the question: “Which types of people were more likely to survive?” using information about passengers such as name, age, gender, and socio-economic status.
